# Managing Team Members

The Team Members section is where you invite people to a team, manage who has access, and remove members when needed. Use it to keep your team's roster up to date as people join, change roles, or leave.

## How it works

You access Team Members from the team itself, not from a separate section of the product. Once open, the section shows the team's current members alongside the tools for adding new ones.

**Any team member can invite new members, approve email domains, and remove existing members.** These actions are not restricted by permission level — coordinate with your team before making changes so the roster stays intentional.

## Open Team Members

1. Locate the team in the left-side navigation.
2. Click the **three dots** on the right side of the team.
3. Select **Team Members**.

This opens the Team Members section for that team.

## Invite a teammate by email

Invite individuals directly by entering their email address. This is the most direct way to bring someone onto a team, and works well when you're adding a small number of people whose addresses you already know.

## Invite people by approved email domain

For larger groups, you can approve specific email domains to join the team automatically. Anyone whose email address matches an approved domain can join without needing an individual invitation.

This is useful when:

- You want a whole company or department added to a team without inviting people one at a time
- You're regularly onboarding new hires who share the same email domain
- You want to reduce the friction of getting new teammates set up

## View and remove existing members

The Team Members section also shows a list of everyone currently on the team. From this list, you can remove a member when they no longer need access — for example, when they change roles or leave the team.

## Best practices

- Use individual email invitations for small, targeted additions.
- Use approved email domains when you expect a recurring flow of people from the same organization to join.
- Review the member list periodically and remove anyone who no longer needs access, so the team's roster stays accurate.

## Things to keep in mind

- Any team member can invite new members, approve email domains, and remove existing members — regardless of their permission level.
- Approved email domains let people join automatically, so be deliberate about which domains you approve.
- Removing a member revokes their access to the team.
- Team Members is accessed from the three-dot menu next to the team, not from a tab inside the team.
- Changing a user's Organization-level permission (Owner / Admin / Member) is done from Organization Team Members, not from a team's own Members section.
