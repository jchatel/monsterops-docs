# Managing Team Members

The Team Members section is where you manage a team's existing roster — viewing who's on the team, approving email domains, and removing members who no longer need access.

> **Trying to invite someone new?** See **Inviting team members** for the full guide. Inviting from the Organization level is preferred because it lets you choose which teams a new member joins; this page only covers managing people in relation to a single team.

## How it works

You access Team Members from the team itself, not from a separate section of the product. Once open, the section shows the team's current members alongside the tools for managing them.

Viewing the member list is open to Owners, Admins, and Members. Two actions in this section are gated to **Owners and Admins**: approving email domains and removing existing members. Members attempting either of these will see a red "Only organization owners or admins can perform this action" popup.

## Open Team Members

1. Locate the team in the left-side navigation.
2. Click the **three dots** on the right side of the team.
3. Select **Team Members**.

This opens the Team Members section for that team.

## Adding members to a team

Adding someone new is covered in its own guide, since the best approach usually isn't from this page. To bring a new person onto a team, see **Inviting team members** — it explains both the Organization-level invite (preferred, lets you choose teams) and the team-level invite, and when to use each.

In short: if you're inviting someone new, start with the Organization-level invite rather than this page.

## Approving email domains

For larger groups, Owners and Admins can approve specific email domains so that anyone with a matching email address can join the team automatically, without an individual invitation.

This is useful when:

- You want a whole company or department added to a team without inviting people one at a time
- You're regularly onboarding new hires who share the same email domain
- You want to reduce the friction of getting new teammates set up

Approving domains is restricted to Owners and Admins. For how this fits alongside individual invites, see **Inviting team members**.

## View and remove existing members

The Team Members section shows a list of everyone currently on the team. The list is visible to all team members.

Removing a member from the team is restricted to **Owners and Admins** — for when someone needs to be taken off the team (for example, when they change roles or leave). Members can see the remove option but will see a red popup blocking the action if they try to use it.

## Best practices

- Review the member list periodically and remove anyone who no longer needs access, so the team's roster stays accurate.
- Use approved email domains when you expect a recurring flow of people from the same organization to join.
- When adding new people, prefer the Organization-level invite — see **Inviting team members**.

## Things to keep in mind

- This page is for managing a team's existing members. To invite someone new, see **Inviting team members**.
- Viewing the member list is open to Owners, Admins, and Members.
- Approving email domains and removing existing members are restricted to **Owners and Admins**. Members will see a red popup if they try.
- Approved email domains let people join automatically, so be deliberate about which domains you approve.
- Removing a member revokes their access to the team.
- Team Members is accessed from the three-dot menu next to the team, not from a tab inside the team.
- Changing a user's Organization-level permission (Owner / Admin / Member) is done from Organization Team Members, not from a team's own Members section.
