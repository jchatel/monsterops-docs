# Using the Team Dashboard

The Team Dashboard gives you a high-level view of your team's activity, priorities, and progress in one place. It helps you quickly understand what needs attention before working in the team's individual sections.

## How it works

When you open a team from the left-side navigation, you enter that team's tabs:

- Team Dashboard
- News
- Rocks
- KPIs
- To-dos
- Issues
- Meetings

The Team Dashboard summarizes activity from these sections without replacing them. Use it as a starting point for reviewing team performance and priorities.

## What the Team Dashboard shows

The Team Dashboard includes a roll-up view of the team's current activity.

### Assigned KPIs

View the KPIs assigned to you within the team. Use this section to monitor the numbers and performance indicators you own.

### Team totals

See the total number of:

- Rocks
- Issues
- KPIs
- To-dos

This provides a quick snapshot of overall team activity.

### Current To-dos

View active team To-dos directly from the dashboard. You can mark To-dos as complete without opening the To-dos tab.

### Top Rocks

View the team's highest-priority Rocks. This section highlights the work currently driving the team forward.

### Top Issues

View the team's most important open Issues that may need attention. Use this section to identify work that could impact progress across the team.

## Using the Team Dashboard

1. Open a team from the left-side navigation.
2. Select the **Team Dashboard** tab.
3. Review assigned KPIs and team totals.
4. Check active To-dos and complete items as needed.
5. Review the Top Rocks and priority Issues.
6. Open other tabs when you need to make detailed updates.

## Best practices

- Start each day in the Team Dashboard to review team activity and priorities.
- Complete To-dos directly from the dashboard to keep work updated.
- Use the Top Rocks section to stay focused on the team's most important work.
- Review priority items regularly to identify blockers early.
- Open the related tab when deeper updates or changes are needed.

## Things to keep in mind

- The Team Dashboard summarizes information from across the team.
- Some updates can be made directly from the dashboard, while others require opening the related section.
- Dashboard content updates automatically as team activity changes.
