# The Organization Dashboard

The Organization Dashboard is the landing page for your Organization — it's what you see when you click the Organization tab in the left-side navigation. It gives you a top-down view of how active the Organization is, who's contributing the most, and how each team is performing.

## How to open it

Click the Organization tab in the left-side navigation. The dashboard opens automatically.

## What the Organization Dashboard shows

The dashboard is split into a few sections, each focused on a different angle of Organization activity.

### Organization level

The Organization's overall level, measured in accumulated **renown points**. Renown points are automatically earned when activities are performed by team members, so the level reflects how much activity the Organization has gone through overall.

Each level has a name and a cap. For example, an Organization might display as **Level 3 - Hollow** with a progress bar reading **89 / 500 renown points** — meaning the Organization has earned 89 renown points toward the 500 needed to reach the next level. The Organization icon you set in **Organization Settings** appears alongside the level.

Renown is a passive indicator. You don't manage it directly, and it doesn't grant any additional permissions.

### Ask anything about your organization

A text box for asking MonsterAI questions about your Organization, surfaced front and center on the dashboard. This is the same MonsterAI you can access from the **MonsterAI agent** in the top right of the screen at any time — the dashboard simply puts it within easy reach when you first open the Organization.

See **Getting help with MonsterAI** for what you can ask and how to get the best results.

### Organization activity

> Daily activity across all teams.

A graph showing daily activity across all teams in the Organization. Use it to see at a glance whether activity is trending up or down, and to spot quiet stretches or busy weeks.

### Top active users

> Ranked by recorded activity across accessible teams.

A leaderboard of the **top 3 users** based on their activity count for the current week. The date range covered is displayed in the top right of the section (for example, *May 20 - May 27*). For each user, you'll see:

- Their **rank** (#1, #2, #3) and avatar
- **Name** and **email**
- The number of **accessible teams** they belong to
- **Last active** date and time
- Their **activity count** for the week

Activity is counted across the teams you can access, so the ranking reflects what's visible to you rather than the Organization as a whole.

### Top active teams

> Hidden or inaccessible teams are excluded from both the ranking and activity totals shown here.

A list of the most active teams. For each team, you'll see:

- The team's **active teammates**
- The team's **activity count**
- The team's **last activity date and time**

As with the user ranking, hidden or inaccessible teams don't appear here — so the picture is scoped to what you can actually see.

## Things to keep in mind

- The Organization Dashboard reflects activity across all the teams you can access. If you're missing teams from view, you won't see their contributions in the rankings or the activity graph.
- Renown points are an activity indicator, not a permission level — they don't unlock features or change what users can do.
- The Top active users ranking covers the current week, while the Organization activity graph and Top active teams show longer-running activity. They're complementary views, not duplicates.
- The "Ask anything" box and the MonsterAI agent in the top right are the same assistant. Use whichever placement is more convenient.
