# Organization Billing and Plan

The Billing / Plan section is where you view your Organization's payment information and the subscription plan it's currently on.

## How it works

Billing / Plan is one of the three admin areas reachable from the three-dot menu on your Organization in the left-side navigation. It contains:

- **Payment method** — the credit card or other payment method associated with your Organization
- **Current plan** — the subscription plan your Organization is running on

## Open Billing / Plan

1. Locate your Organization in the left-side navigation.
2. Click the **three dots** on the Organization.
3. Select **Billing / Plan**.

## Available plans

MonsterOps currently offers two plans.

### Free — $0

The Free plan is for smaller teams getting started with MonsterOps. It includes:

- Up to **10 seats** with all core features
- Run any Business Operating System
- Everything to lead your company from one place
- Low priority email support

### Plus — $129/month (or $99/month billed yearly)

The Plus plan unlocks unlimited seats and the full MonsterAI experience. Annual billing comes with a **20% discount**, dropping the effective monthly cost to $99. The Plus plan includes:

- **Unlimited seats** for teammates, contractors, or coaches
- **MonsterAI™ inside** — get answers about your business directly from the assistant
- **VIP support** from operators who run on MonsterOps daily

## Things to keep in mind

- The Free plan caps your Organization at 10 seats. If you need more, you'll need to upgrade to Plus.
- The Plus plan is billed monthly by default at $129/month, or $99/month when paid annually (a 20% discount).
- Some MonsterOps features — such as importing scorecard history — are limited to paying clients. Your Billing / Plan section is where you'll confirm whether your Organization qualifies.
- For changes to your plan, payment method, or billing cycle that aren't surfaced directly in the product, contact MonsterOps support.
