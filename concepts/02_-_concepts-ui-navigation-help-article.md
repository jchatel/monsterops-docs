# Navigating the MonsterOps interface

MonsterOps's interface is split into two persistent areas — a **left sidebar** for navigation and a **top bar** for global actions. This article maps both so you know where to find every major control.

## The left sidebar

The left sidebar is your primary navigation. Reading top to bottom, it contains:

### Organization selector

At the very top, your current Organization is shown alongside its name and plan tier. Clicking the selector opens a dropdown that lets you switch between any Organizations you belong to, and includes a **+ Create organization** option for starting a new one. See **Creating a new Organization** for the full flow.

### Get Started

The Get Started tab appears just below the Organization selector and shows your current setup progress as a percentage — for example, **Get Started (38%)**. See **Using the Get Started tab** for the full walkthrough.

### Organization

Expands to reveal the three Organization-level sections:

- **Core Values**
- **Org Chart**
- **Objectives**

The Organization tab itself is also clickable — selecting it opens the **Organization Dashboard**.

### Views

Currently contains:

- **Calendar**

### Teams

Lists every team you have access to in the Organization. Each team expands to reveal its tabs:

- **News**
- **Rocks**
- **KPIs**
- **To-dos**
- **Issues**
- **Meetings** (Agendas are contained inside the Meetings page alongside Live Meetings and Past Meetings)

Below the list of teams is a **New team** button for creating additional teams.

### User and account controls

At the very bottom of the sidebar:

- Your **user profile** (avatar, name, email)
- A control for opening **user settings**, switching **language**, and **signing out**

See **Managing your user account** for details.

## The top bar

The top bar runs across the top of every page and contains global controls. Reading left to right:

### Hide sidebar

Collapses the left sidebar to give the main content area more space. Click again to bring it back.

### Current directory

A breadcrumb trail showing where you are in the product — for example, **MonsterOps > Dashboard** or **Leadership > Rocks**. Use it to confirm where you are at a glance, or to jump back up the hierarchy.

### Open MonsterAI Agent

Opens the MonsterAI assistant from any page. This is the same MonsterAI that appears in the "Ask anything about your organization" box on the Organization Dashboard — the top-bar button just makes it accessible anywhere. See **Getting help with MonsterAI** for what you can ask.

### Create item

A **+** button that opens the create dialog for any item type:

- News
- Rock
- KPI
- To-do
- Issue

This is the menu equivalent of the **keyboard shortcuts** (N, R, K, T, I), and works from any page in the product.

### Language

A language switcher with the following options, each shown in its own script:

- **English**
- **Español**
- **Français**
- **Deutsch**
- **Português**
- **日本語** (Japanese)

Changing the language applies across the entire interface.

### Toggle dark mode

A theme toggle that switches between light and dark mode.

### Start timer during a meeting

While hosting a meeting, you'll also see **Start timer** in the top bar. Click it and choose a duration to timebox a discussion. The countdown and its controls appear across all meeting pages and dialog boxes. Click **X** when you're done. See **Using the Meetings tab** for the full walkthrough.

## Tips for finding things

- **If you're not sure where you are**, check the breadcrumb in the top bar. It always shows your current location.
- **If you're looking for an action that creates something**, try the **+** button in the top bar — it covers every standard item type.
- **If you're switching contexts** (e.g., between Organizations or teams), use the Organization selector or the team list in the sidebar rather than navigating away from your current page.
- **If you need MonsterAI**, the agent in the top right is always available, no matter which tab you're on.

## Things to keep in mind

- The left sidebar lists only teams you have access to. Hidden or inaccessible teams won't appear, even if they exist in the Organization.
- The top-bar Create item button and the single-key shortcuts (N, R, K, T, I) open the same create dialogs — use whichever is more convenient.
- The Organization tab in the sidebar and the Organization Dashboard are the same page — clicking the tab opens the dashboard.
- The language switcher affects only the interface, not the content of your items.
